Both marks are redrawn from your description, so proportions are guesses to correct against the original. "Embossed" here is a blind emboss: the leather itself raised, lit from above, no ink. The label is printed rather than hand-drawn: IM Fell English (the map lettering, as in the wordmark), set straight, with no doodles; the explorer writes on its two lines. On the back, the wordmark keeps the fine gear and bulb as its C and I, since letters that size read better with the lighter mark.
