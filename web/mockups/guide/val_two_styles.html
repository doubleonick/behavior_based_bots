Val, drawn two ways

The same Val Brooks (version 3, from above, the caster at the front, the face at the rear), in the two techniques you asked to see. Large as on a page, at map size, and as an icon: Val's controller and its face.

1 · Ink line + soft wash

pen outlines; pale washes, a little out of register

2 · Pencil sketch

your style: lines drawn twice, hatching for dark parts

To weigh:
  • Ink + wash holds up small, keeps the guide's pale colour (earned colour then means stickers and the route are the only bright things), and suits the existing map and creatures with a light redraw. It is drawn by code, so new parts are cheap; it reads more "illustrated book".
  • Pencil is closest to your own pages and the most personal; it reads as a real explorer's notebook, and makes earned colour very clear (everything is graphite until a sticker goes on). It gets busy at small sizes, and every creature and place would need the same treatment (by code like this, or drawn by you and cleaned up like the prologue sketches).
  • Either way, Val's 16-bit pixel art would retire from the pages; it could stay where it belongs, on Val's own 5x5 face.
