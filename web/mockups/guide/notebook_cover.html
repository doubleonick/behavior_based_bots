A notebook cover, from the real one

Black grained leatherette, rounded fore-edge corners, a black elastic band, a pale grey ribbon, cream pages with a pale blue-grey dot grid. Two ways to put the title on it, in six cover colours. Press "Open" to slip the band off and turn the cover.

Cover colour:

The leather grain and creases are drawn by the browser (no photo used), so the cover can scale and stay sharp. Colours are judged by eye from your photos, which were taken under cool indoor light; they will be matched properly once there is a page photographed beside white paper. The grey patterned band along the page's spine side, and the inside of the cover, stand in for the interlocking-square pattern in your notebook: to be redrawn from a photo of it.
