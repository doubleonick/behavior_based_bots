PAGE 2 · STOP 0 · MEET VAL

Meet Val

Val is waking up. It has a face, but it can't feel anything or move yet, a bit like the very first living things.

  • The face is a screen of 25 little lights. It is also Val's only button.
  • The wheel lets Val move, once it has something to move for.
  • The sockets are where Val's senses plug in, later.
[TRY IT] Switch Val on and listen: what does it say? Watch its face. Then learn the one button: short press, long press, hold 2 seconds (the setup cards).
