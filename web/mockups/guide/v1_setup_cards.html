STOP 1 · VEHICLE 1 · SETTING UP VAL

Getting Val ready

Val asks you a few questions, one picture at a time. You answer with Val's screen: it is Val's only button.

Short pressa quick tap: change the answer
Long presshold, count "one", let go: keep the answer
Hold 2 secondswhile Val is driving or resting: start setup again

Before you let Val go

You chose a + wire: more light, faster. Where do you think Val will end up: in the light, or in the dark? Write it in your notebook first.

If you see this box with a cross

Val didn't find a sensor. Check it is plugged in all the way.

A Light Unit needs a grown-up to change one line on the computer first (see the Handbook).

For the adult

Setup starts by itself the first time Val is switched on, and after Hold 2 seconds at any time. Answers are forgotten at power-off unless SAVE_SETUP is on.

Card 3 appears only for a Color Unit. A Light Unit or Reflective IR is found only on Port A, and only when PORT_A_UNIT says it is there.
