FILMING VAL FOR TRACKING · OPTION 1
Overhead, still, plain floor, lamp in shot
From the side
What the phone sees
Floor: a plain, light, matte sheet (poster board or paper), about 90 × 60 cm. No fabric pattern: it confuses the tracking.
Phone: landscape, pointing straight down, held still on a stand, shelf or boom arm (a ruler across two stacks of books works). Main camera (1×), not the 0.5× wide one.
Height: about 70–90 cm, so the whole sheet, the four crosses and the lamp's bright patch all fit in the frame.
Brightness: tap and hold on the sheet to lock focus and exposure, so the picture doesn't brighten and dim as Val moves. Dim the room lights.
Lamp: at the right edge, low, shining across the sheet: bright on the right, dim on the left. Same place for both clips.
Corner crosses: small dark tape crosses at the sheet's corners. They let me straighten any tilt and turn pixels into centimetres.
+ wire clip: start Val near the lamp, facing left. − wire clip: start at the far left, facing the lamp. 10–20 seconds each.
Recording: start recording first, then let Val go, and keep hands out of the shot. Stop after Val stops or leaves the sheet.
