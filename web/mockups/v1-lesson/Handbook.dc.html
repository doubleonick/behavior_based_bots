Stop 1 · The Tide Pools: Getting around

draft, from the Vehicle 1 lesson's notes
What you're finding out
One sense joined to one wheel can look as if it avoids, loves or fears something, though nothing inside does. It's easier to build a behaviour than to work out, from watching, how it's built (Braitenberg's "uphill analysis, downhill invention").
Setting up
Val as Vehicle 1: one module, one servo, one light sensor. Point the Color Unit up, at the light. A bright light on a stand above the floor, on a plain light surface. Set the wire to + or − in setup, then calibrate: low with Val in the dim, high in the light.
Questions to talk about
"Does it ever turn toward or away from anything?" "Why does it end up where it does?" "Woodlice dry out easily. Why might hurrying through bright places help them?"
If something goes wrong
Val hardly changes speed: point the sensor up at the light, and redo the low and high points. [ PLACEHOLDER: more from testing, e.g. what to do if Val creeps when it should stop. ]
Going further
The real name for this is orthokinesis: speed set by what you sense, no steering. Look for it in woodlice and in tiny roundworms (C. elegans slows down on food). Advanced explorers: open Val_Vehicle's code and find where the wire's sum becomes the wheel's speed. [ PLACEHOLDER: more. ]
