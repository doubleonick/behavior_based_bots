Setting up a wire's nerve cell on Val

A mock-up to try, not firmware. Short press changes, long press keeps, as everywhere else on Val. These screens would come after a wire's sign, low point and high point.

    What is assumed here (all open to change): the wire's sign was chosen on the screen before, as today; "no cell" is offered first, so Vehicles 1 to 3 need no extra presses; forward strength sets the cell's gain: 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 for the five shades; the self weight has five choices starting at zero: 0 (no loop), 0.375, 0.75, 1.125, 1.5; the threshold is shown to Val, like the low and high points: hold Val where the cell should start to fire and long press, then try it before keeping it; any level from 0 to 1 is allowed (0: the cell is always awake; 1: it only wakes at the high point); On the real LEDs the two dimmest shades may be hard to tell apart.
