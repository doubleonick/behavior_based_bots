    Tuning the fuzzy colours

    Around the colour wheel (red at both ends), how strongly each channel responds. A channel answers fully at its own hue and fades to nothing at "counts within" degrees away.
    How a colour channel's stimulus is worked out: how close the hue is (the curves above) × how saturated the colour is (white light has no colour) × the light-intensity stimulus from the low and high points. Intensity here is the brightest of red, green and blue, so a red beam counts as bright as a white one of the same strength.
    Vehicle: one channel per Color Unit, chosen first; the sign and the low and high follow, as today. Field: light's sign, then low and high (taken once, on light intensity, shared by every channel), then magenta, yellow and cyan, each starting on x. Only 3 wires can be active; once 3 are, the rest are skipped.
