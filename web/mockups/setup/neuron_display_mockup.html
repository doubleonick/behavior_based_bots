Showing a neuron on Val's matrix: two views

A mock-up to try, not firmware. One light sensor on a + wire. The wiring view shows where the cell sits. The cell view zooms in on that one cell to show how hard it is working.

green: the + wire's spikes cyan: the nerve cell red: holding back white: the wheel
Wiring view. As the spikes display is today, plus one bright cyan pixel: on the lane = in the wire, beside the lane = against the wire. The cell does not change brightness here; its place is the whole message.
Cell view. The cell fills the middle 3 x 3. Spikes come in at the top, the cell's answer leaves at the bottom. The more of the nine squares are lit, the harder it is firing (none lit = silent). What leaves the bottom is green if it drives the motor on, red if it holds the wire back.
Memory. A cell that listens to itself has a loop: its own spikes run from the bottom, up the right-hand edge, and back in at the top. The loop is always faintly drawn, so you can see the cell has one, and cyan spikes run round it while the cell is firing. When the light has gone and the loop is still running, that is the memory.
