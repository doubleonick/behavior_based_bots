# Reference: the owner's notebook

Photos of a real notebook, used as the reference for the guide's notebook look (an
approach being explored, not a decision). Resized to 1600 px. Use them for style and
layout; draw our own pictures rather than copying.

| photo | what it shows |
|---|---|
| `cover_front.jpg` | the cover straight on: black leatherette, rounded fore-edge corners, a pale grey ribbon at head and foot |
| `cover_edge_and_band.jpg` | the closed book: cover overhang, cream page block, the black elastic band |
| `cover_grain_closeup.jpg` | the leatherette close up: fine pebbled grain crossed by a network of fine creases; semi-gloss sheen |
| `ruled_spread.jpg` | a ruled spread: warm cream paper, thin pale blue-grey lines, rounded page corners; the grey interlocking-square (key) pattern on the inside of the covers. (The owner prefers dot grid.) Taken under cool light, so colours read greyer than they are |
| `sketch_val_brooks_v3.jpg` | a real working page: "Val Brooks Version 3: getting spacing and matching right", dated 21 Nov 2026, underlined; a hand-drawn top view of the chassis with corrections in the margin ("only 3 rows", "servo", "M12 nut", "drawn as 8, should be 9", "maybe remove the 'C' icon and use geometric blank space"), and the bottom and top join strips (11 holes long). The model for how the guide's pages could feel |
| `sketch_with_robot_top.jpg`, `sketch_with_robot_underside.jpg` | the same page beside the robot it corrects (the platform the owner tests on now: two M5 360° servos, a Technic caster, the Atom Motion and Matrix on top); also show the key pattern inside the cover and the paper under warm light |

What the guide takes from them is recorded in `FUTURE_WORK.md` (the web guide section)
and tried in `web/mockups/guide/`.
